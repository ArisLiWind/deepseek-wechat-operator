# Product Brief

## One-line positioning

DeepSeek WeChat Operator is a personal Agent layer that turns accessible WeChat information into something you can ask, search, digest, act on, and automate.

## Job to be done

Users do not want to "read more WeChat."

They want:

- the few things worth attention
- the people worth replying to
- the opportunities worth tracking
- the actions worth taking next

## MVP

The MVP should ship five visible actions:

- `Ask`
- `Find`
- `Digest`
- `Act`
- `Automate`

The interface should feel like a command center, not a feature maze.

## Hero feature

Every evening, compress the user's accessible WeChat world into the 10 things that matter.

## Non-goals for v1

- claiming full read access to private personal WeChat history
- claiming complete group surveillance
- claiming official access to all followed public-account history
- using hooks, reverse engineering, or database scraping
